Common Questions

Answers about commercial hood cleaning, compliance, and what to expect from Dirty Deeds Services.

How often should a commercial kitchen hood be cleaned in Michigan?

NFPA 96 sets cleaning frequency based on cooking volume — typically every 1 to 12 months. High-volume restaurants often need monthly service, while lower-volume kitchens may qualify for quarterly or semi-annual schedules. We'll recommend the right interval during your free inspection.

Do you provide documentation after hood cleaning?

Yes. Every service includes before and after photos plus a written service report — ready for fire marshal inspections, insurance records, and internal compliance tracking.

What areas of Michigan do you serve?

We serve commercial kitchens across Michigan, including Detroit, Lansing, Ann Arbor, Grand Rapids, Jackson, and surrounding communities. View service areas.

Is the initial kitchen inspection really free?

Yes. We offer a free inspection to assess your exhaust system, identify grease buildup and compliance risks, and recommend a cleaning schedule — with no obligation.
