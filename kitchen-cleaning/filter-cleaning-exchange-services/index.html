Dirty Filters Create Bigger Problems

Hood filters are one of the most overlooked parts of a kitchen exhaust system. Saturated filters get ignored until they become a problem — reducing airflow, driving grease deeper into ductwork, and creating unnecessary fire risk.

Filter Cleaning & Exchange — Questions

How often should filters be cleaned or exchanged?

High-volume kitchens typically need service every 1–2 weeks. Medium-volume restaurants usually require bi-weekly or monthly service. We'll assess your kitchen and recommend the right interval.

What's the difference between filter cleaning and filter exchange?

Filter cleaning: we remove, degrease, and reinstall your existing filters on-site. Filter exchange: we immediately swap dirty filters for pre-cleaned replacements — no wait time, no downtime.

Do you provide replacement filters?

Yes. We supply professionally cleaned replacement filters during every exchange visit — including baffle filters, mesh filters, and custom sizes for your specific hood system.

What if a filter is damaged?

We identify damaged filters during each visit and provide replacement recommendations — so you're never running on compromised filter media.

Can filter service be combined with hood cleaning?

Yes. Most kitchens benefit from combining filter programs with scheduled hood, duct, and fan cleaning. We build complete exhaust maintenance programs.
