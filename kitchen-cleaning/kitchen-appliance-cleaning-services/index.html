Grease Doesn't Stay Inside the Hood

In Michigan commercial kitchens, grease builds up behind equipment, under cooking appliances, around utility connections, and on surrounding walls. Over time these areas become hard to clean and a serious fire risk.

Equipment deep cleaning removes grease accumulation throughout the Michigan kitchen — not just what's visible from the line.

Equipment Cleaning — Common Questions

How often should Michigan commercial kitchen equipment be deep cleaned?

Most Michigan commercial kitchens benefit from professional deep cleaning every 3–6 months. High-volume concepts should lean quarterly. We assess your operation and recommend the right schedule.

Do you clean Michigan kitchen equipment during off-hours?

Yes. Overnight and early-morning service is standard — we work around your Michigan kitchen's schedule to avoid any disruption to service.

Can dirty equipment cause Michigan health code violations?

Yes. Grease and residue on equipment exteriors, underneath appliances, and on surrounding surfaces are common Michigan health inspection citations. Scheduled deep cleaning keeps you ready year-round.

Are your Michigan equipment cleaning products food-safe?

Yes. We use food-safe commercial degreasers and NSF-listed sanitizers, with proper rinse protocols — appropriate for Michigan commercial kitchens handling food service.

Can equipment cleaning be combined with hood cleaning?

Yes. Many Michigan clients run a complete kitchen maintenance program that includes hood cleaning, filter exchange, equipment deep cleaning, and grease containment on a unified schedule.
