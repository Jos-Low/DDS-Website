Why Proper Hood Cleaning Is Critical

Your exhaust system is one of the biggest risk points in your kitchen. Grease buildup can lead to:

  • Fire hazards
  • Reduced airflow and performance
  • Failed inspections
  • Damage to equipment

Regular, proper cleaning isn't optional — it's necessary.
