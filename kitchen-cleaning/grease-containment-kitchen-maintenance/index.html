What Happens Without Proper Grease Containment

Michigan commercial exhaust fans naturally discharge grease onto rooftop surfaces. Without containment, that grease spreads — and the consequences add up fast.

  • Roofing membrane damage & voided warranties
  • Slip hazards for rooftop HVAC and maintenance crews
  • Pest attraction from pooled grease
  • Fire ignition risk from accumulated fuel
  • Environmental liability from grease runoff
  • NFPA 96 non-compliance

Fan belts wear naturally over time. A worn or failing belt in a Michigan kitchen exhaust fan can cause reduced ventilation performance, excessive noise, vibration, and unexpected system downtime.

We inspect belts during every maintenance visit and replace on a proactive schedule — before your Michigan kitchen loses ventilation capacity.

Michigan Roof Grease Cleanup

Grease contamination doesn't stay at the fan. Over time it spreads across Michigan rooftop surfaces, curbs, utility areas, and adjacent equipment — causing staining, degradation, and slip hazards. Our roof grease cleanup service restores affected areas and prevents ongoing damage.

Grease Containment & Fan Maintenance — Questions

Why is rooftop grease containment required for Michigan kitchens?

Michigan commercial exhaust fans naturally discharge grease onto rooftops. Without containment, grease degrades roofing materials, creates fire hazards, attracts pests, and violates NFPA 96 requirements. Most Michigan kitchens with rooftop exhaust systems require compliant containment.

How often should Michigan grease containment systems be serviced?

Monthly inspections are common for busy Michigan kitchens, with deeper maintenance every 3–6 months based on cooking volume. We'll set the right schedule for your operation.

How do I know if my Michigan exhaust fan belt needs replacement?

Common signs include reduced kitchen ventilation, excessive fan noise, vibration, or visible belt wear. We inspect belts on every visit and replace proactively — before your Michigan kitchen loses ventilation capacity.

Do you install grease containment systems for Michigan kitchens?

Yes. We install new containment systems, replace existing hardware, and provide ongoing maintenance — including absorbent pad replacement and system inspections as part of scheduled Michigan kitchen maintenance programs.

What is a hinge kit and does my Michigan fan need one?

A hinge kit allows the exhaust fan to tip open for safe cleaning and maintenance access. Many Michigan kitchens are missing them or have damaged ones — we identify this during inspection and make recommendations.
